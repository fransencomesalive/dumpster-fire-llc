Profile · Plan & Billing Detail

The popups behind the profile card's Plan and Billing buttons. This is the deliberate, quiet home of the Apply Wizard usage count; it appears nowhere in the dashboard, header, job cards, or the wizard itself. Plan shows the plan, price, allowance, uses remaining, reset date, export availability, and change-plan. Billing handles renewal, cancellation, and the Stripe Customer Portal. Change flows cover upgrade (immediate, prorated), downgrade (at renewal), cancel + reverse, and past-due recovery.
