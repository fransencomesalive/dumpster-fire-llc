Header & Navigation

One bar, one grid, two sets of contents, and it is one line. The layout is the live production header and does not change: mascot lockup, then the links row, then the actions slot on the right.

Signed out is unchanged: Features, Human Path, Pricing, Contact, and the Sign in / Create profile pair.

Signed in changes only what sits in those two slots. The links row becomes Job scan, Saved Pursuits, a divider, then Plan and Billing. The actions slot becomes the email and the profile icon, which opens Sign out. Both dropdowns are .selectMenu, the picker this app already ships. Menu items carry the bar's own underline and its reg-shadow hover. Home points at the profile page. Plan and Billing open their popups in place, over whatever page you are on. The email truncates with an ellipsis so it can never push the icon out of the bar.

Below 900px the signed-in links move into a hamburger rather than wrapping, so the bar is one line at every width down to 320. The signed-out bar keeps the behaviour it has in production today.
