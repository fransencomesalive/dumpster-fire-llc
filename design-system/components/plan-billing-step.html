Onboarding · Plan & Billing Step

The /plan step between sign-up and onboarding. Everyone lands here; a signed-out homepage pick lands here preselected. Two paid plans only — Smoldering ($22) and Roaring ($32, top plan) — in parity with the homepage pricing cards. Billing facts and Terms consent live on the plan cards, so Start goes straight to Stripe's secure checkout. Access is unlocked by the signed Stripe webhook, never by the browser returning to a success URL. Testers satisfy the step with an access code and never touch checkout. This card covers acquisition; account management (upgrade, downgrade, cancellation, past-due) lives on the Plan & Billing detail card.
